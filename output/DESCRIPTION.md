**Title:** The Salad Oil Test: how Buffett priced fear (1963)

**Description:**

In 1963, a fraud built on tanks of "soybean oil" that were mostly water cut American Express stock nearly in half. Most people asked how bad the news was. Buffett asked a sharper question: was the damage to the balance sheet, or to the franchise?

He tested it in the field. He watched diners pay at an Omaha steak house and checked with banks on travelers cheques. Trust was intact. The claims were large but finite; the franchise was durable. He built the position to about 40% of his partnership.

The lesson isn't the return. It's the method:
A falling price is a fact. A falling value is only a hypothesis. Go test it.
